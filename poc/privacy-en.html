Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

At BrightMinds we respect children's and parents' privacy, and we are committed to protecting data and not using it in any unsafe way.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect sensitive personal information from children such as full name, address, or phone number.

We may store simple information on the device only, such as:

  • The child's name (if a parent enters it)
  • Age
  • The child's in-app progress

This data stays on the user's device and is not sent to any third party except when required to run a subscription (e.g., payment via Stripe).

2. How we use information

We use the data only to improve the learning experience:

  • Tailoring exercise difficulty to the age
  • Showing the child's progress
  • Improving the app experience

3. Sharing data

We do not sell or share children's data with any third party.

We only use trusted service providers like Stripe for secure payment processing.

4. Children's protection

The app is designed for children, therefore:

  • No ads
  • No ad tracking
  • No inappropriate content

5. Parent rights

Parents can, at any time:

  • Delete the child's data from the device
  • Reset the app completely

6. Contact us

If you have any question, please contact us at:

support@brightminds.kids
