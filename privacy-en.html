Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026 • Effective: May 24, 2026
✓ COPPA-compliant ✓ GDPR-K compliant ✓ No ads ✓ No tracking
The short version: BrightMinds collects zero personal information from your child. Everything (name, age, progress) is stored only on their device and never sent to us or any third party.

1. About this policy

BrightMinds (the "App" or "we") is designed for children ages 5–13. Because we serve children, we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States and Article 8 of the GDPR (GDPR-K) in the European Union.

2. Who is responsible?

Operator: The BrightMinds team
Parent contact: privacy@brightminds.kids
General contact: support@brightminds.kids

3. What data we collect from the child

Nothing reaches our servers. Here's exactly what happens:

DataWhere stored?Can we see it?
Child's name (optional)Browser only (localStorage)No
Child's ageBrowser onlyNo
Game progress and starsBrowser onlyNo
Sound / language settingsBrowser onlyNo
Parent PINBrowser (SHA-256 hashed)No

We never request or store: child's email, phone, address, photos, voice recordings, location, or any other identifying data.

4. What about parent data?

If a parent subscribes to Premium, our payment provider (Stripe) collects the following from the parent only, never from the child:

  • Email address (for billing receipts only)
  • Payment details (processed by Stripe — we never see them)

We retain only: email + subscription status (active/expired) in Vercel KV. We do not share this data with any third party for marketing.

6. Advertising

There are no ads in BrightMinds — not now, not ever. We do not allow any ad networks to access children.

7. Parent rights (important)

As a parent, you have the right at any time to:

  • Review your child's data (it's on your device — open Settings)
  • Delete all child data (Settings → "Reset all progress")
  • Refuse further use (delete the app — all data is erased)
  • Cancel Premium subscription anytime (active until end of paid period)
  • Request a copy of your subscription data: email privacy@brightminds.kids

8. Verifiable parental consent

The app is completely free for kids and collects no data from them, so no prior consent is required for basic use.

When subscribing to Premium, we require explicit confirmation that you are a parent aged 18+ before redirecting to Stripe.

9. Security

  • The site always uses HTTPS (full encryption)
  • Parent PIN is hashed with SHA-256 (never stored as plain text)
  • Security headers: HSTS, X-Frame-Options, Content-Security-Policy
  • We security-review every third-party library before integration

10. Data retention

  • Child data: as long as it stays on the device — we keep no copy
  • Parent subscription data: up to 7 years after cancellation (accounting + CFRA requirements)

11. International data transfers

Vercel + Stripe servers may be in the United States or Europe. Every transfer occurs under EU-approved Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

12. Changes to this policy

Any material change will be announced in-app and the "Last updated" date above will change. Changes take effect 30 days after posting, except for urgent fixes.
