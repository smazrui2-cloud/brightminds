🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026
In one sentence: BrightMinds collects no personal data about your child. Everything is stored on their device only, never sent anywhere.

1. What data do we collect?

Nothing leaves the child's device. Child's name, age, progress, stars, words learned — all saved in localStorage inside the device's browser only. We never see it and cannot access it.

2. Do you use cookies or tracking?

No tracking cookies, no analytics that identify users. We may use Umami Analytics (cookieless, anonymous) to count visitors only.

3. Do you share data with third parties?

No. Never. We don't sell data, share it, or send it to any company.

4. Who uses the app?

Designed for kids ages 5-13. We encourage parents to supervise usage and adjust device settings as needed.

5. Ads

No ads in the app, ever. We want a safe environment for kids.

6. Your rights

  • Right to delete your child's data anytime (Settings → "Reset")
  • Right to know what data we have about you (answer: nothing — it's all on your device)
  • Right to export child's data (coming soon)

7. Children under 13 (COPPA)

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA US + GDPR-K EU). We never request email, phone, address, or any identifying info about the child.

8. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, the new version will be posted here with an updated date.
